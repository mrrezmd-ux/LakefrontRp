Lakefront RP — Faction Community Rules

0 — Melee Weapons & Expectations

0.1 Definition

Melee weapons are meant to create tension, intimidation, or realistic self-defense during RP. They are not intended to be used as free-kill tools or for constantly hunting rivals.

0.2 Proper Usage

Use melee weapons only when the situation and roleplay reasonably support it, such as realistic self-defense, intimidation, or a sudden fight. Properly roleplay where the item came from.

0.3 Prohibited Usage

  • Do not constantly carry visible melee weapons without an RP reason.
  • Do not hunt rivals around the map with melee weapons.
  • Do not use melee weapons as a faction’s primary attack method.
  • Do not force unrealistic outcomes.

0.4 Situational Exceptions

Sudden emotional violence may be acceptable only when the existing RP clearly supports the reaction and it remains realistic.

0.5 Rule Intent

Melee weapons should improve scenes and realism, not turn faction RP into nonstop combat.

1 — Faction Standards & Representation

1.1 Faction Portrayal

All factions must represent their background, culture, clothing style, behavior, setting, and timeline in a believable way.

1.2 Slang & Culture

Players do not need perfect slang, but factions should maintain a believable culture and tone.

1.3 Leadership Responsibility

Faction leaders are responsible for ensuring members understand server rules, escalation standards, and faction culture. Poor management may result in strikes or closure.

2 — Definition of a Faction Attack

2.1 What Counts as a Faction Attack

A faction attack occurs when members knowingly enter rival territory or approach rivals with hostile intent, including organized retaliation or other planned hostile actions.

2.2 What Does Not Count

Passing through an area, peaceful social RP, or meeting at a public business without hostile intent is not automatically a faction attack.

2.3 One-on-Ones & Brawls

  • Agreed one-on-one fights are not faction attacks.
  • If additional people join and the situation becomes faction-related, it is treated as such.
  • The side that escalates the situation may be considered the aggressor.

2.4 Outside Turf Situations

Existing faction conflict remains faction-related even when the interaction happens away from a faction’s normal area.

2.5 Remaining in Rival Turf

Do not linger in rival areas after an attack without a legitimate RP reason. Leave when the scene naturally ends unless RP continues.

2.6 “Deep” Rule

Entering deep into rival territory with multiple vehicles or large groups requires valid RP reasoning. Do not pull up simply to provoke conflict.

3 — Cooldowns & Sliding Rules

3.1 Sliding Cooldowns

  • After attacking another faction, the attacking faction has a 1-hour general cooldown from attacking other factions.
  • The attacking faction has a 12-hour cooldown with the same faction.

3.2 Retaliation

The attacked faction may retaliate when it has proper IC proof of who was responsible. After retaliation, both factions enter a 12-hour mutual cooldown and a 1-hour general cooldown.

3.3 Fight Cooldowns

  • Personal fights unrelated to gangs: no cooldown.
  • Gang-related one-on-ones: 15-minute cooldown.
  • Brawls/jumpings: 15-minute cooldown.

3.4 Robbery Cooldowns

  • After robbing rivals: 1-hour cooldown with that faction.
  • If retaliation occurs: 4-hour mutual cooldown.

3.5 Block RP

After a faction attack, the attacking faction must remain on its own block for at least 40 minutes of RP before beginning another aggressive action.

4 — Escalation & Engagement

4.1 Escalation Purpose

Conflict must build naturally through RP. Factions cannot instantly escalate from an argument to a major violent conflict without buildup.

4.2 Passive Tension Phase

Tension may develop through believable interactions such as verbal disrespect, graffiti/tagging, mocking rivals, or minor conflict.

Required Proof: 1 screenshot and 2 logged interactions before escalating into fights.

4.3 Fighting Phase

Physical altercations must have a story purpose. Random jumping with no buildup is punishable.

4.4 Robberies & Stripping

Before robbing rivals, factions need 3 screenshots and 5 negative interactions. Any stripping must be properly roleplayed with /me and /do.

4.5 Post-Robbery Cooldowns

A 1-hour cooldown begins after a robbery. Retaliation places both factions into the applicable mutual cooldowns.

4.6 Shooting Beef Requirements

Before sustained faction gunplay, there must be 9 documented interactions and 4 screenshots showing ongoing hostility. Conflict must develop naturally from ongoing RP.

4.7 Violence Must Support RP

Faction conflict should remain story-focused rather than score-focused. Random deathmatching or kill-focused behavior is prohibited.

5 — Behavior & Engagement Rules

5.1 Provoking

Constantly circling rival areas or provoking people without a legitimate RP purpose is prohibited.

5.2 Public Events & Safe Areas

Faction violence and conflict are prohibited at server events and safe zones.

5.3 Civilians

Civilians who involve themselves in faction politics accept possible IC consequences.

5.4 Allied Factions / Cliquing

Factions may assist allies when the enemy is mutual and the numbers remain realistic. Do not stack excessive numbers simply because allies are nearby. Players who are alone may choose not to participate in faction conflict and may prioritize their character’s safety.

6 — Faction Management Rules

6.1 Ceasefires

Ceasefires should happen naturally through IC roleplay and may require faction management approval.

6.2 Rebranding & Turf Changes

Factions may not suddenly rename, relocate, or rebrand without approval.

6.3 Report Window

Faction reports must be submitted within 24 hours of the incident.

7 — Enforcement

7.1 Faction Punishments

Poor escalation, unrealistic behavior, random violence, or repeated rule breaks may result in warnings, strikes, suspensions, or faction closure.

7.2 Leadership Accountability

Leaders are responsible for the quality of their faction’s RP and member behavior.

7.3 Server Integrity

Faction RP should create long-term storylines, character development, and realistic conflict. Conflict should support roleplay instead of replacing it.
